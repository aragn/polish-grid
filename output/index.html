Data: PSE S.A. reporting API, reports gen-jw (metered output of JWCD units, average MW per quarter-hour) and pdwkseub (available capacity, declared non-usable capacity and grid limits, published for about half the units) (field map). Day-ahead price: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (Poland bidding zone, €/MWh; hourly until 30 Sep 2025, quarter-hourly since), fetched daily after the auction. The “Other” layers are PSE's system balance (his-wlk-cal) less the JWCD units: total generation is jg + jnwrb (scheduled units plus units outside the balancing market), wind and solar are wi and pv. Other wind and Other solar subtract Baltic Power and Zwartowo; “Other thermal, hydro, biomass” is the rest less the JWCD thermal units as PSE nets them (jgw1) and the pumped-storage output (PSE gives no fuel split for it). PSE's totals are net of the plants' own use and the JWCD layers are gross meter readings, so the top of the stack sits above PSE's total generation by the JWCD plants' own use (about 0.7–1 GW). Energy is MW × 0.25 h per quarter-hour; pumping is counted separately and subtracted for the net. Capacity is available + non-usable + grid limit, the most over the dates shown. Fuel, operator and place are this site's plant-level labels (from the TGE unit register and operators' own pages), not the fuel burnt in a given quarter-hour, and can be wrong. Times are Warsaw time; on the October clock change the repeated hour is marked CEST and CET. Code on GitHub.
