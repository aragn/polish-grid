What Poland's big power plants generate

Metered output of every centrally dispatched unit (JWCD) on PSE's transmission grid, quarter-hour by quarter-hour, with the pumping of the two pumped-storage plants, the rest of the system, load and cross-border flows; today and tomorrow from PSE's day-ahead plan. Read live from PSE's public API; the meter data come in during the day, a few hours behind.

Data: PSE S.A. reporting API, reports gen-jw (metered output of JWCD units, average MW per quarter-hour) and pdwkseub (available capacity, declared non-usable capacity and grid limits, published for about half the units) (field map). Day-ahead price: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (Poland bidding zone, €/MWh; hourly until 30 Sep 2025, quarter-hourly since), fetched daily after the auction. The “Other” layers are PSE's system balance (his-wlk-cal) less the JWCD units: total generation is jg + jnwrb (scheduled units plus units outside the balancing market), wind and solar are wi and pv, and the top of the stack is PSE's total generation. Other wind and Other solar subtract Baltic Power and Zwartowo. PSE gives no fuel split for the rest, so it is split with ENTSO-E's actual generation per type (A75, which PSE submits): each type less the JWCD units of that fuel, scaled to PSE's rest (“Other coal” is hard coal and lignite; “Other oil, coal gas, other” is oil, coal-derived gas, other renewable and other). Where ENTSO-E has not yet published a quarter-hour the rest stays one layer, “Other thermal, hydro, biomass”. Load is PSE's system demand (his-wlk-cal, demand); “Net imports (physical)” is the metered net cross-border exchange (swm_p + swm_np), drawn above generation when Poland imports and below zero when it exports. The dashed line and the paler “Plan” layers are PSE's day-ahead coordination plan (PKD, pdgopkd, published about 15:40 the day before): the load forecast, and the planned supply of scheduled units, other units, wind, solar, net exchange and storage charging. The planned layers fill only quarter-hours without meter data (today and tomorrow); the planned supply can exceed the load in sunny, windy hours, where PSE plans a surplus. Energy is MW × 0.25 h per quarter-hour; pumping is counted separately and subtracted for the net. Capacity is available + non-usable + grid limit, the most over the dates shown. Fuel, operator and place are this site's plant-level labels (from the TGE unit register and operators' own pages), not the fuel burnt in a given quarter-hour, and can be wrong. Times are Warsaw time; on the October clock change the repeated hour is marked CEST and CET. Code on GitHub.
